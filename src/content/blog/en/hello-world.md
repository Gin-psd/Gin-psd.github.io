This is the first post on my new blog.

For years my technical notes were scattered across bookmark folders and local directories — hard to find, and rarely revisited. The motivation for this site is simple: **write things down so they stick, and have something to show for it**.

## What to expect

- **Tech notes** — pitfalls, tools and workflows worth remembering
- **Project retrospectives** — what went right, what went wrong
- **Reading & thinking** — non-technical topics in the long run

## About this site

Built with [Astro](https://astro.build/) — no client-side framework overhead, everything is Markdown. It supports bilingual (zh/en) switching, dark mode, full-text search and RSS.

The source is on GitHub. Feel free to take a look.
